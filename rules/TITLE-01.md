id: TITLE-01
title: Product title format
kind: guidance
trigger: Every product
action: Write a descriptive title from the supplier data, following the pattern "<Brand>® <strength> (<active ingredient>) <form> for <species> <use or condition>" when those facts exist; leave out parts that don't apply. Title case for words, but active ingredient names stay lowercase. Never ALL CAPS, never supplier codes, packaging words or abbreviations from the supplier name (e.g. "F-Style", "w/View-Strip"). Size is not part of the title.
example: Supplier name "TiaGard 1-Liter F-Style w/View-Strip" -> "TiaGard® 12.5% (tiamulin hydrogen fumarate) Liquid Concentrate for Swine Dysentery & Pneumonia Treatment"
reason: Consistent, descriptive titles help search and collection pages.
