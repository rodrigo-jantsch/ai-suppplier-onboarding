id: TITLE-01
title: Product title format
kind: guidance
trigger: Every product
action: Title = "<Product Name>"; title case; no ALL CAPS; no supplier codes; Allow lower case ingredient names.
example: "TiaGard® 12.5% (tiamulin hydrogen fumarate) Liquid Concentrate for Swine Dysentery & Pneumonia Treatment"
reason: Consistent titles help search and collection pages.
